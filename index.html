The Real Problem

Your pet can't tell you
when something's wrong.

By the time symptoms show up at the vet's clinic, kidney disease, diabetes, or GI disorders have often progressed silently for weeks — hidden inside eating and drinking habits no one was tracking.

⚠️

Symptoms appear too late

Chronic conditions develop gradually. Without intake data, early warning signals are invisible — even to trained vets.

📋

Vets diagnose blind

Vets rely on owner memory: "she eats normally, I think." One week of real gram-level data changes the entire diagnostic picture.

💧

Water intake is critically undertracked

A cat needs ~30–40ml of water per kg per day. Subtle deviations are one of the earliest indicators of kidney and urinary disease.

How It Works

Set it once. It works forever.

No calibration rituals. No behaviour training for your pet. KIRA is passive — it just watches so you don't have to.

1

Place the unit

KIRA sits below your pet's food and water bowls. Load cells measure weight silently and continuously.

2

Pet approaches

Motion sensor triggers camera and load cell sampling. A timestamped photo and intake delta are logged instantly.

3

Data builds up

7–8 days of gram-level trends give your vet a complete picture — food intake, water intake, meal frequency, timing.

4

Vet gets the report

Export a clean report before your appointment. Or connect your vet directly to the KIRA dashboard.
